SIEM stands for Security Information and Event Management. It is a software tool that collects log and security data from your entire network, cloud, and devices, analyzes the information in real time, and alerts teams to potential cyber threats.  
Core Functions of a SIEM 

• Log Collection: Gathers data from servers, firewalls, and endpoints into one central place. 
• Normalization: Sorts and standardizes different log formats so they match. 
• Event Correlation: Links separate events together to spot hidden attack patterns. 
• Alerting: Warns security staff when suspicious behavior happens. 
• Compliance: Creates reports to help pass audits and meet rules.
