5. High Availability & Fencing Configuration

Fencing & CRM Architecture: The cluster operates with corosync_votequorum (3 votes, 2 required for quorum). The Master CRM runs on prox22 with hardware/softdog watchdog fencing armed on all nodes. In case of unexpected host disconnection, the active CRM watchdog guarantees split-brain protection and restarts protected VMs on surviving nodes.
