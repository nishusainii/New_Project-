Your planned HA data flow is:

VM runs on prox22/tank
          |
          | scheduled ZFS replication
          v
Replica on prox23/tank
          |
prox22 fails
          |

Because ZFS replication is asynchronous, changes made after the last completed replication may be lost during a sudden node failure. 
Proxmox explicitly notes that HA can be combined with storage replication, but data loss may occur between the last synchronization and the node failure.
